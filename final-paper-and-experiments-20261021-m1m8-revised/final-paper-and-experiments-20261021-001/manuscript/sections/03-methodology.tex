\section{Scope and Systematization Method}\label{sec:methodology}\label{sec:background}
\subsection{Research questions, eligibility and threat polarity}
We organize the systematization around four questions: which hidden targets, threat polarities and auxiliary observables appear across LLM and agent systems (RQ1); how signal generation origin relates to receiver observation permissions (RQ2); which holdouts establish portability across tasks, configurations and hardware (RQ3); and which evidence binds workflow protection, logical state preservation and resource cost, distinguishing these from full end-to-end planner utility (RQ4).

Core inclusion requires five conjunctive criteria: (C1) LLM or agent execution, (C2) a hidden target property, (C3) an execution-dependent auxiliary observable rather than ordinary output semantics alone, (C4) specified observer permissions, and (C5) an empirical inference evaluation. Mixed-feature papers qualify only if they isolate an auxiliary-only C3 component; thus coding fingerprints, WildAuth, ZeroPrint, endpoint-content fingerprints and StepFinder remain adjacent boundary comparators \cite{ghaleb2026codingfingerprint,wang2026dialogue,shao2026zeroprint,leshin2026endpoint,zhu2026stepfinder}. \subsection{Corpus construction and reconciliation}
We construct a purposive corpus through 40 seeds, venue-targeted proceedings screening and one-hop citation snowballing, supplemented by database and bulk keyword searches. The supplementary search returned 615 occurrences and 565 unique records; auditing 204 exclusions rerouted four records, yielding 15 new core and seven adjacent families. The final collection has \FinalCore{} core and \FinalAdjacent{} adjacent families. The Boolean queries overlap 25/60 previously indexed families (41.7\%); this is a warning about query coverage of a known seed set, not an estimate of field-wide recall. The other discovery routes contribute known families missed by those queries. Findings describe this assembled corpus, not prevalence across all literature (search accounting: S1/S10).

\subsection{Coding procedure and consistency}
AI assistance produced initial schema-guided field drafts. Authors checked and corrected all 39 result records and the 180 archived audit cells against source section/table/figure/paragraph locators, and reviewed all 82 family classifications using titles, abstracts and experimental sections. These version-bound locators, rather than an agreement coefficient, support inspection of the claims. The archived draft-to-author-verification agreement is non-independent; its historical statistics and all disagreements are retained as process diagnostics in S8/S18. No independent human inter-rater reliability is claimed. Later AI-assisted source reconciliation is separately identified and never promoted to an independent human rating.
