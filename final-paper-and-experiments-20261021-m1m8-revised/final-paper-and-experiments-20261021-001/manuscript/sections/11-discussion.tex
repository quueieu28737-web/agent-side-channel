\section{Three Literature Case Studies}\label{sec:discussion}
Record-level synthesis resolves three deployment decisions: whether upstream shaping survives another workflow hop, whether cover preserves the relevant state, and whether adaptive evaluation and cost describe the same mechanism. The following cases provide the source-local evidence for each judgment.

\subsection{Case 1: bind the final target-bearing emitter}
\paragraph{The downstream re-emission boundary}
NetEcho's upstream packet-size shaping is an Origin~B result; it does not bind the Origin~A tool-call sequence. Its plaintext-aware frontend then emits a new Origin~B trace (grouped token counts or character lengths), reopening a size channel after upstream protection \cite{netecho2025}. SplitDecoy is a separate return-path record: the cloud sees nonzero gradient row support on every tested frame across nine seeds \cite[Sections 5.1--5.3]{politis2026splitdecoy}. The inference is a changed receiver boundary, not one transferable score across B and A.

The standard data processing inequality applies when $S\to Z\to Y$ is a Markov chain: target-oblivious postprocessing cannot increase $I(S;Z)$ \cite[Theorem~2.8.1]{cover2006elements}. A plaintext-aware frontend or unprotected backward computation changes that premise. The systematization insight is identifying which published records cross this boundary.

\subsection{Case 2: resolve the WRA versus AHB cover asymmetry}
\paragraph{Two axes of asymmetry: threat polarity and action risk}
A comparison that groups WRA's decoy requests \cite{jeong2026wra} and AHB's humanized actions \cite{zhu2026turing} together as ``cover traffic'' can create an apparent paradox: WRA reports a clean privacy improvement (one concurrent decoy reduces functional-intent reconstruction $E_{\rm func}$ from 0.770 to 0.610), whereas AHB reports severe task collapse (Trip Planning success falls from 0.75 Raw to 0.15 under combined \texttt{Online+Fake} humanization).

Both records originate in Origin~A actions, but polarity and state effects differ: WRA's concurrent decoy retrievals are discarded reads for Type~I confidentiality, while AHB's live GUI events evade a Type~III platform detector and can navigate or submit forms. Their utility outcomes are not contradictory, and AHB's missing condition-specific denominators preclude a matched causal effect. Origin~A alone predicts neither receiver permissions nor whether an action mutates protected state.

Terminal-state reward does not establish dispatch-time authorization: an unconfirmed write could happen to produce the task's intended final state. For stateful tools, audit the real execution path and protected account/database, not only the terminal hash. Exp~2 (\S\ref{sec:exp-state}) tests this distinction with simulator mutations.

\subsection{Case 3: bind adaptive retraining and cost objects}
\paragraph{The $D_0\to D_2$ rebound and component-cost boundaries}
A matched adaptation contrast compares an undefended-data-trained attacker $D_0$ and a defended-data-trained, defense-aware attacker $D_2$ on identical defended tests. UI Traces' timestamp-delay refitting and Dangol's stored/live distinction show why receiver and run provenance must accompany the score \cite{uitraces2026,dangol2026workflow}. Exps~1--2 supply direct contrasts where $D_0$ reaches chance and $D_2$ recovers the signal; Exp~4 expands the attacker families.

On the cost side, Early Bird's $6$ms/$4\%$ GPTCache overhead does not price $K$-prefix isolation; KVGov's $1.3\,\mu$s HMAC microcost omits lost cache reuse; and SpliceDefense's QCP cost does not price combined CTBF+QCP \cite{earlybird2025,addagada2026kvgov,spliceleak2026}. CachePrune binds attack suppression, QA/summarization utility and throughput; OTRO reports access-count leakage conditional on byte length and separately scoped TTFT/padding cost \cite{wu2026cacheprune,lee2026otro}. Even within Origin~A, identical call order is not a state guarantee: read/write traces can retain the same dispatch features while writes alter the account or database. The state claim therefore requires execution-path and final-state checks (Exp~2), not the Origin label or trace score alone.
