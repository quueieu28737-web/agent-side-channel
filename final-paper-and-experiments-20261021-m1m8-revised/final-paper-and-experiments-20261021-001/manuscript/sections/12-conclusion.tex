\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This SoK turns published claims into source-bound comparison records. It identifies failures that paper-level grouping obscures: protection lost at a new plaintext-aware emitter, read cover reused across a state-changing boundary, and costs or utility borrowed from a different mechanism. Our own measurements expose the same mistakes: action-defined labels limit what write localization establishes, preserved replay state does not measure planner utility, and inexpensive transport adaptations match costly shadow shaping on selected measured point metrics. The practical output is an inspectable map of justified and unjustified reuse, with matched measurements where available and precise missing obligations where deployment claims remain unsupported.
