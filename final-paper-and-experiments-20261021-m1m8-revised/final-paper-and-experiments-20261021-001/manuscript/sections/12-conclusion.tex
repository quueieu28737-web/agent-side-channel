\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This SoK organizes \FinalN{} study families and 39 intervention result records into an audit of receiving projections, execution boundaries and matched evaluation objects. Its contribution is to show which comparisons the existing measurements support, which conflict, and which remain unresolved. The author-constructed scenario exercise illustrates the rules without establishing external decision accuracy.

Pre-registered transfer tests show that the checks flag transfers that then fail. A buffered cap does not price a token stream. Randomized and decoy cover leave cancellation and return intent inferable. Cover evaluated at a dispatcher leaks through request sizes on the encrypted tool link. Only the joint binding of execution and transport approached chance, at substantial cost. These replays do not establish protection for autonomous agent workflows. The reusable result is the evidence boundary and the specific additional measurement each transfer claim requires.
