\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This SoK organizes \FinalN{} study families and 39 intervention result records into an audit of receiving projections, execution boundaries and matched evaluation objects. Its contribution is to show which comparisons the existing measurements support, which conflict, and which remain unresolved. The author-constructed scenario exercise illustrates the rules without establishing external decision accuracy.

Controlled boundary tests illustrate concrete limits on reuse; they do not validate the audit's predictive discrimination. A buffered cap does not price a token stream. Randomized and decoy cover leave cancellation and return intent inferable. Leakage already present at a dispatcher persists through record-size decoding on the encrypted tool link. Several cells have near-chance point estimates, but no network configuration meets the evaluated-suite criterion for both cancellation and return. These replays do not establish protection for autonomous agent workflows. The reusable result is the evidence boundary and the specific additional measurement each transfer claim requires.
