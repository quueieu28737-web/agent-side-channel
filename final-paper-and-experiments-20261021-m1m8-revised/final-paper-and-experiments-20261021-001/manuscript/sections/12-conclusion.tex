\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This SoK connects \FinalN{} study families and 39 scoped result records to an executable evaluation of LLM and agent side-channel defenses. The audit identifies which observation, execution and evaluation bindings govern a result's reuse; controlled experiments demonstrate the leakage and cost consequences of changing those bindings.

The complete 690-run real-agent study provides a concrete deployment lesson. Workflow rules achieve 97.1\% measured compliance while intent inference exceeds 93\%. Training-calibrated online shaping reduces baseline network BA by 47.2/44.0 points to 50\% at 15.4$\times$ bytes and 3.8$\times$ calls. Intent lives in state-changing calls that rules leave untouched; bucketed framing cuts the byte cost by 37\% at matching or lower leakage.
