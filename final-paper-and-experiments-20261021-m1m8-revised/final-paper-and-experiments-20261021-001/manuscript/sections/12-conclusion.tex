\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This SoK connects \FinalN{} study families and 39 scoped result records to an executable evaluation of LLM and agent side-channel defenses. The audit identifies which observation, execution and evaluation bindings govern a result's reuse; controlled experiments demonstrate the leakage and cost consequences of changing those bindings.

The complete 690-run study identifies a concrete design target. Rules attain 97.1\% mechanical rule adherence while intent inference exceeds 93\%; an observer ablation localizes the dominant signal to state-changing calls. Training-calibrated shadow shaping lowers baseline network BA by 47.2/44.0 points to 50\% in the length/order view of TLS replay. In the cross-fitted comparison, bucketed framing reaches 52.2\%/52.7\% BA while reducing byte cost from 19.8$\times$ to 12.4$\times$. A further 4,140 live tool executions bind arrival timing and actual waiting cost to this comparison, including a complete tunnel that emits cover traffic without invoking tools. Together, the source-level audit and executable measurements guide defenses whose protected observation, preserved state and deployment cost can be evaluated on a common workload.
