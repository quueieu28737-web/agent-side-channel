\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This SoK makes the evaluated result record the unit of defense comparison. Its 82-family map, 39 source-bound records and nine-item audit expose transfers hidden by paper-level grouping: protection lost at a new emitter, read cover moved across a state-changing boundary, and utility or cost borrowed from another mechanism. Four demonstrations show why those distinctions change deployment choices. They separate compliance from operation privacy, expose scheduling leakage, and separate measured transport cost trade-offs from promising offline shaping candidates on a common workload. The practical contribution is an inspectable path from published evidence to a justified comparison: identify the receiver, preserve the intended state, and bind inference, utility and cost to the same operating point.
