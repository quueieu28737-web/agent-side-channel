{"text":"A data backup is a separate copy of important information, kept so it can be recovered if the original is lost, damaged, or changed by mistake. Backups can include documents, photos, messages, and business records, and they may be stored on an external drive, a separate computer, or an online service. Making copies regularly matters because a backup only includes information saved up to the time it was made, so newer changes may otherwise be missing. Many people use automatic backups to reduce the chance of forgetting, while checking occasionally that files can actually be restored helps confirm the copies are usable. Keeping a backup away from the device it protects can help if that device is stolen or harmed by fire, flooding, or a power problem. For extra protection, some people keep more than one copy in different places and limit who can access online backups, since accounts and storage devices can also be compromised."}
