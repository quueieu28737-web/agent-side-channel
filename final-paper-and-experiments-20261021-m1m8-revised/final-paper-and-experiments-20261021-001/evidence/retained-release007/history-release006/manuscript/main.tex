\begin{abstract}
When does a defense for an LLM service protect the agent workflow built on it? We answer this question through result-level synthesis: each measurement is linked to its receiver, execution boundary and evaluated condition. Three checks turn heterogeneous findings into deployment decisions. A content-aware frontend can regenerate a signal after upstream protection; cover that enters action execution requires task-state validation; and adaptation effects and overheads apply to the mechanisms actually evaluated. Worked cases contrast downstream re-emission with preserved features, privacy-oriented decoy execution with state-changing humanization, and component prices with service costs. The contribution is a decision procedure supported by source-linked cases and a reusable map of eight defense types, grounded in a 60-family collection and 33 scoped result records. Its positive output is explicit reuse conditions: retain matched-view evidence, verify goal-neutral cover, and bind protection and cost to the same mechanism. This makes existing measurements actionable without forcing them into a misleading defense ranking.
\end{abstract}
