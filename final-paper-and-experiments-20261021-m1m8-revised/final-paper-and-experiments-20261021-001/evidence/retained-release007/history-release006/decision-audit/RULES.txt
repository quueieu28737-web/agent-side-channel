Result-comparison audit, 2026-10-21

Purpose and authority
This is a retrospective, AI-assisted source-coding audit of 33 already-selected
result records, not a new experiment, an independent human validation, a
preregistered study, a literature prevalence estimate, or an author-error rate.
No original measurement or frozen input is modified. Compatibility concerns the
specified inference, not the quality of a paper. The same two records can support
one inference and fail to support another.

Units and relevance (fixed before aggregation)
The complete inventory contains all 528 unordered pairs of distinct records.
Mechanism-question cohorts, assigned from the source mechanism before the
comparison verdict, define the related subset: traffic-envelope mitigation,
cache-service mitigation, behavioral humanization, local-memory mitigation,
generator fingerprint variation, and fixed guardrail/instruction variation.
Access control, contention noise and filter-created auxiliary observations are
retained as singleton contextual cohorts. These are purposive relevance bins,
not claims that their endpoints coincide. They are mutually exclusive solely to
avoid counting a pair twice. Same-family pairs are separately reported.

Inference D: descriptive comparison
Question: may the two records be used as source-qualified observations about the
same hidden-property / receiver-facing outcome, explicitly retaining each
metric, mode and test population, WITHOUT numerical ranking or subtraction?
Compatible: a coded positive endpoint-and-permission alignment supports that
restricted comparison. Topic classification at different granularities can be
described together, but neither its accuracies nor AUPRC are equated.
Incompatible: the requested SAME-OBJECT comparison substitutes a component cost
for an inference outcome, a different protected property, or a demonstrably
different signal/permission object. It remains permissible to discuss these as
different components; this verdict forbids only pretending they measure the same
thing. Unresolved: the needed endpoint/permission relation is not established.
Exact text equality or inequality is never the rule. The semantic mapping and
positive conflict rules are explicit in semantic-coding.csv and the script.

Inference P: paired effect
Question: may one subtract the two records' outcome estimates as a paired
intervention effect on the same defended test population? Applicability requires
an aligned measured endpoint and a plausible same-study test population; unrelated
studies are not fabricated into prospective paired experiments. Compatible
requires affirmative matched-test / fitting evidence. Known distinct collection
or execution populations are incompatible. Missing matching/fit bindings are
unresolved. D-compatible does not imply P-applicable or P-compatible. Internal
clean/adapted contrasts already within one record are counted separately and
must not disappear because this inventory compares distinct records only.

Inference T: deployment transfer
Question: may an effect/cost obtained under the source record's conditions be
reused unchanged at the destination record's condition? Direction is explicit.
For related pairs, a positively documented wrong endpoint/component/primitive
blocks that proposed transfer. An absent empirical bridge alone is unresolved,
never incompatible. Compatible requires a source-backed positive bridge covering
the claimed endpoint and range, not source similarity or membership in one paper.
The pair table reports each direction separately. There is no pooled transfer
success rate over all 528 pairs. Source-local licenses are not cross-record
transfer and are not counted as such.

Six-check family audit
Denominator: exactly the 16 families whose frozen status is VERIFIED_EVALUATION.
Every family has six cells linked to source scope and result IDs. SAT means the
item is supported for the source's scoped claim. PARTIAL means supported elements
and an explicit unbound element coexist. NR-R means the inspected source scope
does not report the needed element; NR-V means a source/run/version binding is
unverified. NA means the requested kind of inference is not made/applicable.
No blank-to-NR conversion and no total family quality score are permitted.
C1 hidden target and protection relation.
C2 receiver-visible fields and linkage permissions (collector fields distinguished).
C3 protection placement and whether cover executes (offline explicitly identified).
C4 protected-data access, fitting, matched defended test when an adaptive-protection
inference is at issue. For fitted classifiers or calibrated thresholds, a later
deployment claim about adaptation needs this binding even if the original paper
did not claim adaptation resistance. NR-V does not accuse that original paper of
failing to test a claim it never made. The two fitted attacks newly recognized in
this audit (Speculation and TimeWillTell) are therefore kept in the applicable
binding inventory rather than declared inherently incapable of fitting.
C5 workloads/generator shifts actually tested, not all conceivable future shifts.
C6 inference, objective/policy utility and applicable cost under the SAME condition.
Component-only evaluations can satisfy their narrow scope while leaving C6 partial
for a deployment inference. This is not an assertion that every original study
owed a complete agent deployment experiment. Subfields separately retain utility
kind and cost object; proxy usefulness never becomes objective task success.

Verification
The script validates 33 unique unit IDs, 22 families, 528 unordered pairs,
symmetry/direction bookkeeping, all 16 expected evaluated families, 96 checklist
cells, source-file existence and frozen source SHA256, row locators, unique
coverage, nonblank reasons, and immutable input hashes before/after derivation.
Counts are descriptive deterministic tallies. No confidence interval, p-value,
causal effect, diagnostic sensitivity, or SCOPE-vs-checklist superiority is
estimated by this audit. All available/source-limited distinctions remain visible.

Coding disclosure
The family audit uses one AI-assisted workflow with parallel source-reading
assistants. They are not independent human coders, and their agreement is not
reported as validation. Check numbers1-6 are not the manuscript's C1-C5 eligibility
conditions. At the whole-cell level NR-R can be zero while specific utility/cost
subfields are NR-R, because a PARTIAL cell retains both present and absent items.
An unmeasured component-to-deployment bridge is not proof of poor component design.
