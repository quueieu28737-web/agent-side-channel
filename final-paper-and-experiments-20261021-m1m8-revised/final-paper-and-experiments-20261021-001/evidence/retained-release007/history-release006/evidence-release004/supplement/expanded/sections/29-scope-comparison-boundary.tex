\section{Workflow Comparison and Claim Boundary}
\label{app:scope-workflow-boundary}
This model-assisted finite release-contract task is not expert literature review or validation of surveyed attacks. Arms share ten complete checks, evidence, semantics and schema: R self-organizes a flat checklist; T uses extract--check--reconcile; V uses SCOPE indexing and receiver-oriented traversal. T may also use graphs, joins and revisits; V has no privileged information or exclusive inference rule. Fresh \texttt{gpt-6-luna}/low sessions randomize arm order.

The fixed battery contains 512 primary roots derived from UI Traces pairs, eight constructed graph/query templates, and 18 separate FP-Agent source-file groups. Graphs, receiver permissions and transformations are researcher-created controls, not original-paper mistakes, deployed defenses or new agent trajectories. Source-file groups are not authenticated people or independent study families. Sixteen development roots are excluded. For root $i$, arm $a$ and query $q$, $J_{iaq}=1$ only when the decision, exact equal/possible-configuration counts, supplied identifiers and shared schema all pass. The root score is $S_{ia}=\frac13\sum_{q=1}^3 J_{iaq}$; its three queries are not independent observations.

\paragraph{Randomization, missingness and envelope}
Within each root, an independently randomized permutation assigns R/T/V to its three request positions; the saved schedule is the replay record. Fresh sessions receive the same evidence and request limits. Let $\widehat\Delta=512^{-1}\sum_i(S_{iV}-S_{iT})$. Conditional on fixed slot potential outcomes and no cross-request interference, the independent root-level contrasts lie in $[-1,1]$. The two-sided Hoeffding bound gives
\[
h=\sqrt{2\log(2/0.05)/512}=0.12004035.
\]
One T root is unscored. Ranging its whole score over $[0,1]$ gives $[\Delta_L,\Delta_U]=[-0.13151042,-0.12955729]$ without assuming missing at random. The reported envelope is the union over those possible scores,
\[
[\max(-1,\Delta_L-h),\;\min(1,\Delta_U+h)].
\]
Its target is the fixed battery's slot-averaged contrast under the assignment assumptions, not a population of papers, people or models. Provider-state interference remains unverified. The released script and root/query inputs reproduce both the original scores and this arithmetic without new calls or fits.

All $(512+18)\times3=1,590$ scheduled slots were admitted; 1,589 are scorable. The service-overload HOLD retains unknown usage; 1,552 previously unstarted slots were admitted under the recorded amendment. Known usage is 6,848,613 tokens plus the unknown slot. No failed gate is relabeled passed. The contrast is negative under both missing-score extremes and does not support the specified $+10$pp advantage. The separate 18 source-file groups give $-9.26$pp descriptively; the earlier 24-case pilot is not pooled.

\paragraph{Post-outcome stratification}
Each of eight templates has 64 primary roots: six have negative V-minus-T differences, one zero and one positive. All packets have 11 nodes, 11 edges, maximum depth four and four global configurations; there is no graph-scale experiment. Receiver-ancestor cone size varies but is entangled with template. The supplemental root/query tables retain all template, cone, relevant-variable and equality-count strata. These post-outcome descriptions do not identify a causal complexity mechanism or add confirmatory tests.

\paragraph{Post-outcome method and input review}
Internal mechanical checks found no discrepancy in the declared finite semantics, not construct validity: an irrelevant binary domain preserves the decision but doubles required counts. The endpoint mixes judgment and enumeration. The 512 alpha-distinct packets cover only 377 equality patterns/eight templates. Flags in 343 source pairs neither certify corruption nor justify deleting failures.

Post-outcome descriptive views retain a negative V-minus-T difference: $-12.43$pp on the 169 roots without those flags and $-12.56$ to $-12.29$pp with one root per equality pattern. These are not replacement primary analyses, and the flag-free subset is not certified task success. No raw records are deleted or outcomes replaced. The checks and references were produced and reviewed with AI assistance, not independently validated by researchers.

\paragraph{Consequence for the SoK}
The tested workflow shows no advantage over the information-matched checklist, not general framework invalidity or human usability. Our tables are systematization outputs, not demonstrated productivity gains.
