\section{Intervention Evidence and Applicable Costs}
\label{app:defense-comparison}
Table~\ref{tab:defense-comparison} is a compact family summary; each execution/component mode is a distinct unit in the 33-row supplemental result ledger. It is not a homogeneous paper-level effect table or the full \FinalCore{}-family disposition table. The 16 evaluated families, two fixed guardrail conditions, four other interventions, thirteen scoped negatives, four diagnostic N/A and four unresolved bindings are defined in Table~\ref{tab:intervention-status}. The five multifeature-trace subset is separate from Early Bird's calibrated-oracle stage.

Cost objects are mechanism-specific: token payload and plaintext padding are not wire bytes; model loading and table lookup are not added inference latency. D1/D2 do not establish feasible labeled-data access or matched adaptation budgets. The supplemental 66-position utility/cost inventory retains each source, scope and disposition, not 66 measured endpoints. R denotes the target quantity unreported in that scope, not a pending lookup, zero overhead or evidence of preserved utility.

\paragraph{Disposition of unbound claims}
AgentPrint's referenced supplementary defense result is not bound to an evaluated unit. Weiss's confirmation and Gu's provider changes establish disclosure context, not matched quantitative defense effects. PromptEcho reports approximately 3.2$\times$/1.4$\times$ padding overhead without a bound measurement method and defended outcome. These four claims are withheld from verified defense-effect comparisons, not recoded as failed defenses \cite{agentprint2025,weiss2024prompt,gu2025promptcaching,prompt_echo2026}. Whisper Leak's values below remain source-reported; original-run reproduction is not claimed.
