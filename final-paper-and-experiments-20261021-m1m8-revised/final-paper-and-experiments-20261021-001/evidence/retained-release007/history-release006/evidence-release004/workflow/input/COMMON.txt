  histogram returns [token,count,token,count,...] in numeric token order.
- Nodes are a DAG but may appear in any order. Resolve their references, not
  their printed order. A variable op is the configured domain value.
- Every joint configuration is a Cartesian-product choice from ALL domains.
  Configuration domains express unreported settings, not random priors. They
  apply identically to both states and all queries. Do not invent new settings.
- A receiver's final view is its ordered list of slots. For a permitted slot
  emit the referenced node's full result; otherwise emit null. A variable access
  uses the configured Boolean. Receiver labels alone grant no other access.
- For each query count configurations with EXACTLY identical final left/right
  views. If all are equal answer SUPPORTED, if none are equal answer REFUTED,
  otherwise answer INSUFFICIENT. Unknown fields can be irrelevant to a query;
  known unequal surviving data can refute equality despite other unknowns.
