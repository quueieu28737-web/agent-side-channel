Method T: complete generic structured audit

Use the complete common instructions. Cover K01 K02 K03 K04 K05 K06 K07 K08 K09 K10.
Phase one is extraction. Make private notes of the requested statement and all
supplied facts, including references, domains, permissions and possible missing
information. Distinguish observed values from claims and separate the supplied
finite task from any general privacy conclusion or source-paper attribution.

Phase two is checking. For each query inspect the relevant items against every
common check. Compute required quantities and organize a claim/evidence/status
table if useful. Resolve cross-record connections and follow references even
when their printed order differs. Mark conflicts and missing facts; determine
whether either actually changes the answer rather than assuming uncertainty.

Phase three is reconciliation. Compare the individual checking results, resolve
dependencies or contradictions, and look for overlooked surviving outputs and
counterexamples. Revisit earlier items whenever a later fact changes their
meaning. You may use tables, graphs or calculations; a correct global join is
allowed. Verify the final decision, exact counts and evidence identifiers.

Finally return the shared JSON schema without any additional method report.
The phases organize work; they do not add facts or restrict correct solutions.
