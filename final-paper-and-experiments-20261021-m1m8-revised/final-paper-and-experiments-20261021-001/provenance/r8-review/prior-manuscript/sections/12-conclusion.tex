\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
This SoK binds published defense results to their receiver, execution boundary and evaluation conditions. Separating records reveals why upstream shaping, read-only cover, matched adaptation and component costs cannot be transferred interchangeably. The real-agent analysis identifies state-changing calls as a dominant signal and measures how shaping changes inference at substantial cost. Timing-aware evaluation and classical traffic-defense comparisons further delimit that effect. The reusable contribution is the evidence framework and its mechanism-level findings: evaluate the protected observation, preserved state and cost on the same workload, and distinguish paired improvement from an unconfirmed absolute bound.
