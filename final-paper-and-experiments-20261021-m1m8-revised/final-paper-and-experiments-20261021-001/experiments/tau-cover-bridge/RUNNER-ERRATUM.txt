The continuation runner's initial docstring says previously started cells are
never executed again. The precise, verified guarantee is narrower: every cell
already recorded in the first attempt is carried unchanged and not executed by
the continuation. The interrupted worker may have had uncommitted in-flight
local work; that identity/completion status is not known and is not guessed zero.

There were no model, network or external-service calls in either local task
execution phase. The completed census contains one saved result for each of
3450 unique task/seed/position/arm keys. Any uncommitted computation is not an
additional independent observation. The frozen runner bytes and first attempt
are retained; this erratum corrects the wording rather than rewriting history.
