Saved-results interpretation checks (11/11), 2026-10-21

1 Simpson reversal: before/after are reported separately. Reference-error-free
  and cover-eligible subsets accompany the full census; no pooled sign reversal
  is used to select a narrative. Native reward is lower under live cover in
  both positions and each reported subset.
2 Ecological inference: the unit is a pinned retail task/reference program.
  No inference about human coders, autonomous models, other frameworks or
  all deployed agents is drawn from these task aggregates.
3 Selection/Berkson: all115 tasks remain in the census, including18 without
  an eligible pending-order cover. Conditional subsets are separately labelled.
4 Collider: subsets use initial cover eligibility and errors in the untouched
  reference program, not success under a treatment. No outcome-based filtering.
5 Base rates: no privacy classifier is evaluated. Write effects, state equality
  and reward use their named task/cell denominators; these are not attack PPV.
6 Regression to mean: treatment arms share the same fixed program and matched
  seed/order. Tasks were not selected for extreme baseline model performance.
7 Survivorship: all3450 unique cells are retained. The stopped attempt's851
  saved cells are carried unchanged; reference-error cases remain visible.
8 Look-elsewhere: all five arms, both positions and frozen endpoints are saved.
  Intervals are descriptive; no multiple-testing-adjusted discovery is claimed.
9 Forking paths: protocol and original source hashes precede collection. The
  scoring-cache continuation is disclosed. It changes computation reuse, not
  task choice, cover assignment, endpoints or the native reward. Unknown
  uncommitted local work at interruption is disclosed in RUNNER-ERRATUM.txt.
10 Causality: fixed-program routing contrasts support effects in this controlled
   backend. They do not establish privacy benefit or real-world policy-violation
   prevalence. Unconfirmed cover is part of the constructed experimental context.
11 Reverse causality: cover insertion is fixed before/after the reference program;
   the saved state transitions follow the assigned routing intervention.

Confidence intervals resample whole task IDs, keeping three seeds and paired
arms together. These characterize task-composition resampling on a fixed test
suite. Degenerate intervals for unchanged control routes do not certify future
systems. Gold textual outputs hold the native output predicate fixed; reward1
under reference replay is not autonomous task accuracy. The19 reference programs
that return tool errors are reported, not silently labelled invalid or removed.

The three copied benchmark data tables were independently fetched from the
same immutable upstream commit and matched byte-for-byte. Every one of244
unique task/final-state combinations was reconstructed and rescored with the
original native reward. This numerical check is not independent human review.
