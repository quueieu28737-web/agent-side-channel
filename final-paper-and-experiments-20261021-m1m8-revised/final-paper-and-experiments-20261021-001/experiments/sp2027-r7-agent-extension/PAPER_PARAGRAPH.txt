Using the subscription channel, we collected 150 fresh GPT-6 Luna/low retail trajectories on a fixed convenience subset of 75 existing tasks, with separate planner and user-simulator contexts. Baseline and standardized prompts achieved 32/75 and 33/75 native rewards. Per-prompt attackers used 29 training and 46 test trajectories from 10/24 customer-disjoint groups; test cancellation/return had five/seven positive customer clusters. Retrained dispatch-suite BA under fixed inherited classifier settings was 0.666–0.892 for writes-only and 0.500–0.573 for prefix-filtered reads-only. Every reads-only upper confidence bound exceeded 0.60. This filter is narrower than the full non-write complement. The fixed gradient booster was constant with 29 training rows, limiting power. Results concern a same-provider model on previously used public tasks, without establishing cross-provider transfer, new-customer generalization or independent human validation.
